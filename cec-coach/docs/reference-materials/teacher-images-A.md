## WhatsApp Image 2026-06-16 at 9.32.07 PM (2)

**Type:** Code/handbook figure — cable protection at framing members.

**Transcription (OCR):**

> Note: Hole size must be adequate to facilitate pulling in cable without damage.
>
> **Figure 12-30  Cable protection**
>
> - Protector plate approved for the specific purpose
> - "32 mm or more"
> - "Less than 32 mm"

**Notes:** This is the CEC Figure 12-30 illustrating protection of cables run
through holes bored in studs/joists. The rule of thumb shown: when the edge of the
bored hole is **less than 32 mm** from the nearest edge of the framing member, a
**protector plate approved for the purpose** is required to guard the cable against
nails/screws; when the cable is **32 mm or more** from the edge, the protector plate
is not required. (Corresponds to CEC Rule 12-510 / Figure 12-30 cable-protection
requirements.)

  near the top.

**Notes:** Illustrates the maximum support/securing spacing for the cable from the

securing cables near boxes). The "300 mm maximum" is the distance from the box to
the first cable support.
