Basement room expansion — under-deck enclosure

Project Description

Removal of the existing basement exterior wall (including the sliding glass door) and construction of three new exterior walls under the deck (front wall ~19 ft with two double-pane windows and one entry door; two side walls ~9.5 ft each); conversion of the existing deck into the room's roof, including salvage removal of the composite decking, sloped sheathing, a walkable waterproof membrane, and re-installation of decking and railings; thermal insulation of walls and roof; new foundations; stucco exterior matched to the building; complete electrical, heating/ventilation and interior finishes. Added floor area approximately 180 sq ft. All work per BC Building Code 2024.

BC Building Code Compliance (BCBC 2024)

  • Foundations are mandatory: exterior walls must bear on footings and foundations (Section 9.15) with bearing below the depth of frost penetration (9.12.2.2). Placing the walls on the existing patio slab is therefore not permitted, and items 4–7 are an inseparable part of the project.
  • Seismic: West Vancouver is in a high seismic zone; wall bracing and connections are built per 9.23.13.
  • Batt-insulated roof joists: the space above the insulation is ventilated in every joist bay (Section 9.19) via continuous vents at both ends of each bay; the poly vapour barrier is installed on the warm side (9.25.4).
  • Energy: R-22 effective walls and windows/door at USI ≤ 1.61 meet or exceed Section 9.36 requirements.
  • Room height: the ~9 ft ceiling is well above the Section 9.5 minimum.
  • Radon: sub-slab rough-in per 9.13.4 (province-wide requirement in BCBC 2024).
  • Fire and life safety: smoke alarms interconnected with the whole house (9.10.19) and CO alarm (9.32.3.9).
