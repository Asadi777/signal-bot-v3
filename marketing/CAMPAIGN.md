# BC Star Academy — Instagram Marketing Campaign

**Brand:** BC Star Academy (a division of BC Star Electric)
**Website:** www.bcstarelectric.ca
**Instagram:** @electricalworkshops
**Product:** Free / freemium exam-prep web app for the BC **Construction Electrician** (**Red Seal** / **C of Q**) exam on **CEC 2024**
**App features:** 440 practice questions with full step-by-step solutions · timed mock exam · per-section lessons · Keyword→Table map · Code-Hunt drills · AI tutor
**Audience:** Persian (Farsi) speakers across Greater Vancouver who are electricians or aspiring electricians preparing to challenge the exam.

---

## 1. Brand Voice & Positioning

BC Star Academy speaks like a **trusted electrical instructor** — calm, experienced, and genuinely on the learner's side. The tone is warm and approachable, never condescending. We are the colleague who has already passed the exam, who knows exactly where people get stuck in the **Code**, and who is happy to sit down and walk you through it. Every sentence should feel like a real teacher talking, not an ad.

Our positioning is simple: **we help the Persian community of Greater Vancouver pass the Construction Electrician exam and build real careers in the trade.** We combine two things competitors rarely have together — a serious, **CEC 2024**-accurate study tool (440 questions, mock exam, Code-Hunt, AI tutor) and a team that explains it all in fluent Persian while keeping the technical terms in English, exactly the way the exam uses them.

Credibility comes from BC Star Electric itself: a working residential and commercial electrical company in Greater Vancouver. We are not a faceless course — we are tradespeople teaching the trade. That dual identity (real electricians + real teachers) is our biggest differentiator and should show up across the feed.

---

## 2. Audience & Insight

**Who they are**
- Persian/Farsi speakers living in Vancouver, North & West Van, Burnaby, Coquitlam, Port Moody, Richmond, Surrey, New Westminster, and Maple Ridge.
- Newcomers and established immigrants, ages roughly 22–55: some already work as electricians elsewhere on their ticket, some are apprentices, some are career-changers trying to break into the trade.
- Many intend to **challenge** the BC Construction Electrician exam rather than go through a full apprenticeship.

**Their pain**
- The exam is **open-book**, which sounds easy but is brutal: without knowing *where* a rule lives in **CEC 2024**, you run out of time flipping pages.
- **Language barrier** — studying complex Code in English while thinking in Persian slows everything down and shakes confidence.
- **Expensive courses** and prep programs that don't speak their language or fit their schedule.
- Isolation — no Persian-speaking instructor to ask "why is the answer this Table and not that one?"

**The promise**
> You will learn to find any rule in **CEC 2024** in seconds, practice on 440 real-style questions with full solutions, and walk into the exam calm and prepared — taught in your own language by electricians who've done it. Start free.

---

## 3. Content Pillars

1. **Exam Tips & Strategy** — open-book technique, time management, how to use the index, the Keyword→Table map, mock-exam mindset.
2. **Code Rule of the Week** — one practical **CEC 2024** rule explained simply (e.g., bonding, ampacity, box fill, GFCI/AFCI requirements).
3. **Success & Motivation** — encouragement, study routines, "you can do this," community wins (with consent).
4. **Behind the Trade** — real job-site moments from BC Star Electric, tools, what the work actually looks like, day-in-the-life.
5. **Services** — residential/commercial electrical work in Greater Vancouver (soft, occasional; keeps the brand grounded and brings local leads).

**Suggested pillar mix per week:** Exam Tips ×2, Code Rule ×1, Motivation ×1, Behind the Trade or Services ×1.
